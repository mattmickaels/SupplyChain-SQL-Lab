New to SQL? Start here

SQL is a language for asking questions about data stored in tables. You do not need programming or supply-chain experience.

Start with lessons 1 and 2 to select columns, filter rows, and sort results. Continue with lessons 3 and 4 to summarize data and combine tables. Lessons 5 and 6 build confidence with dates and missing values.

Lessons 7 to 10 are optional next steps: advanced queries, planning problems, and timed practice. You can leave them until the basics feel comfortable.

Read a worked example, then try one exercise at a time. Run shows your results; Check tells you whether they answer the question. Mistakes are safe: lessons cannot change the data.
